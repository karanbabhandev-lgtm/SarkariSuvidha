💳 PAN Card Kaise Banaye – Poori Jaankari

Naya PAN Card apply karna hai, e-PAN download karna hai ya PAN me correction karna hai — sabki step-by-step process, zaroori documents aur official government link yahan diya gaya hai.

✅ 100% Online Process 📄 Free e-PAN Available 🔗 Sirf Official Portal

PAN Card Kaise Banta Hai

  1. 1Portal ChuniyeProtean (NSDL) ya UTIITSL – dono government-authorised portal hain. Aadhaar-linked mobile number hai to Income Tax e-Filing portal se free Instant e-PAN bhi ban sakta hai.
  2. 2Form BhariyeNaam, jaanm tithi, address, Aadhaar number aur mobile number sahi-sahi bharein. Application type "New PAN – Indian Citizen (Form 49A)" select karein.
  3. 3Documents Upload KareinIdentity proof, address proof aur date of birth proof scan/upload karein (details neeche di gayi hai).
  4. 4Aadhaar OTP se e-Sign KareinAadhaar-linked mobile par aaye OTP se application verify/e-sign karein — physical documents bhejne ki zaroorat nahi padti.
  5. 5Fees Pay KareinDebit/credit card, net banking ya UPI se application fees pay karein.
  6. 6Acknowledgement Number Save KareinIsi number se apna PAN application status track kar sakte hain.
  7. 7e-PAN Milegae-PAN aapke registered email par kuch ghanto/dino me aa jaata hai. Physical card chuna hai to woh dawak (post) se ghar aata hai.

Kaun Kaun Se Documents Lagte Hain

🪪 Identity Proof (koi ek)

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Voter ID Card
  • Driving Licence
  • Passport

🏠 Address Proof (koi ek)

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bijli/Bank ka bill (recent)
  • Passport
  • Bank Passbook / Statement

🎂 Date of Birth Proof (koi ek)

  • Birth Certificate
  • 10th Class Marksheet/Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport

📸 Extra Zaroorat

  • Passport size photo (2 copies, offline ke liye)
  • Active mobile number (Aadhaar se linked)
  • Valid email ID
💡 Note: Agar aapka Aadhaar mobile number se linked hai, to zyada tar cases me sirf Aadhaar hi teeno documents (identity, address, DOB) ke liye kaafi ho jaata hai.
