Voter ID Card Kaise Karein

  1. 1Eligibility Check KareinGeneral voter registration ke liye Indian citizen hona aur qualifying date par 18+ hona zaroori hai.
  2. 2Voter Services Portal KholeinECI ke official Voters' Services Portal par login/sign-up karein.
  3. 3New RegistrationNew voter ke liye Form 6 online fill karein.
  4. 4Documents Submit KareinPortal par required details/documents upload karein.
  5. 5Application StatusApplication submit hone ke baad portal se status track karein.
  6. 6Name Search KareinElectoral roll me apna naam aur voter-related details search karein.
  7. 7Correction / ReplacementExisting elector ki correction, address shifting ya EPIC replacement ke liye Form 8 use karein.
