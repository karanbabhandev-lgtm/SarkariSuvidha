👴 Old Age Pension Bihar – Poori Jaankari

Bihar ke old age pension scheme me naya application, e-KYC, payment status, dues status aur beneficiary list check karne ke official links aur process.

👴 Old Age Pension💰 Monthly Payment📋 e-KYC / Status

Old Age Pension Kaise Lein

  1. 1eLabharti Portal KholeinBihar Sarkar ke eLabharti portal par visit karein ya niche diye gaye official link par click karein.
  2. 2Naya Pension Apply KareinNew Application / Apply Old Age Pension option par click karke online form bharein.
  3. 3Documents Upload KareinAadhaar, bank passbook, photo aur other required documents upload karein.
  4. 4e-KYC Complete KareinApplication ke baad e-KYC mandatory hai; Aadhaar se biometric / OTP based e-KYC complete karein.
  5. 5Payment Status Check KareinBeneficiary status se monthly payment kab aayegi, kitni payment mili hai aur kitna dues hai yeh check karein.
  6. 6Beneficiary List DekheinApna naam list mein hai ya nahi yeh district/block/village se check kar sakte hain.
