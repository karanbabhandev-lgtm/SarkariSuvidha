PM Kisan Kaise Karein

  1. 1PM-KISAN Portal KholeinPM-KISAN ki official website open karein.
  2. 2New Farmer RegistrationPortal par New Farmer Registration service select karke required details fill karein.
  3. 3Aadhaar VerificationRegistration process me portal ke instructions ke according Aadhaar/details verify karein.
  4. 4e-KYC Complete KareinPM-KISAN registered farmers ke liye e-KYC mandatory hai; official available method use karein.
  5. 5Beneficiary Status CheckRegistration number/required details se payment/account status check karein.
  6. 6Beneficiary List DekheinState, district, sub-district, block aur village select karke beneficiary list check karein.
