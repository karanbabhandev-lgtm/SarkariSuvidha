Aadhaar Card Kaise Karein

  1. 1 UIDAI Portal Kholein Aadhaar services ke liye UIDAI/MyAadhaar official portal open karein.
  2. 2 Enrolment / Update Naya Aadhaar ya update ke liye authorised Aadhaar Seva Kendra/Enrolment Centre ka use karein.
  3. 3 Status Check Karein Enrolment/update ke baad required details se status check karein.
  4. 4 e-Aadhaar Download Aadhaar number ya Enrolment ID ke through electronic Aadhaar download karein.
  5. 5 PVC Card Order Official MyAadhaar portal se Aadhaar PVC Card order karein.
  6. 6 Details Update Address aur other permitted updates ke liye official UIDAI process follow karein.
