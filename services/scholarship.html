National Scholarship (NSP)

National Scholarship Portal se all-India scholarships.

Bihar ke students ke liye alag-alag level aur category ke hisaab se teen mukhya scholarship portals hain. Neeche har portal ke baare mein jaankari di gayi hai ki kaunsa portal aapke liye sahi hai aur kaise apply karna hai.

📚 1. Post Matric Scholarship (pmsonline.bihar.gov.in)

Ye portal un students ke liye hai jo Class 11 ke baad ki padhai kar rahe hain (Intermediate, Graduation, Post-Graduation, Diploma, ITI etc.), especially SC/ST/OBC/EBC/Minority category ke students ke liye.

  1. Official website kholein: pmsonline.bihar.gov.in
  2. Naya student registration karein apne Aadhaar aur mobile number se.
  3. Login karke apni educational aur bank details bharen.
  4. College/Institute verification ke baad application submit karein.
Post Matric Portal Par Jaayein →

🏫 2. 10th / 12th / Graduation Scholarship (Medhasoft)

Ye portal Bihar ke School aur College level ke students ke liye hai — jaise Mukhyamantri Balika/Kanya Utthan Yojana aur anya schemes jo 10th, 12th, aur Graduation students ke liye hain.

  1. Official website kholein: medhasoft.bihar.gov.in
  2. Apni scheme/category select karein (school ya college ke hisaab se).
  3. Student details, marksheet details aur bank account details bharen.
  4. Application submit karke reference number save kar lein.
Medhasoft Portal Par Jaayein →

🇮🇳 3. National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

NSP Central Government ka portal hai jo poore India ke students ke liye scholarships offer karta hai — Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, Merit-based, aur minority scholarships included. Bihar ke students bhi apni eligibility ke hisaab se yahan apply kar sakte hain.

  1. Official website kholein: scholarships.gov.in
  2. "New Registration" par click karke apna account banayein.
  3. Login karke apni scholarship scheme select karein aur application form bharen.
  4. Documents upload karke apni institute se verification karwayein.
NSP Portal Par Jaayein →

📋 Common Zaroori Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Account details (Aadhaar linked)
  • Latest Marksheet
  • Income Certificate
  • Caste Certificate (agar applicable ho)
  • Bonafide/Admission Certificate apne institute se

⚠️ Important Baatein

  • Sabhi scholarship applications free hain — koi fees nahi lagti.
  • Application deadline se pehle apply karein, last date miss na karein.
  • Bank account details bilkul sahi bharein, warna scholarship amount transfer nahi hoga.
